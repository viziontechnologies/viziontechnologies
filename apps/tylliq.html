01 — Setup

Getting started, from key to checkout.

Six steps from "just signed up" to running Tylliq on every till you're licensed for.

1

Get your license key

After you sign up, we send your business a license key that looks like LIC-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX. It's the credential that unlocks Tylliq for your business — treat it like a password and don't share it outside your team.

2

Install Tylliq on your first till

Download and open Tylliq POS on the computer, tablet, or terminal you'll check out customers on. When it asks for a license key, enter yours.

3

Activate the device

Tylliq checks your key against our system and unlocks itself for that specific device. You'll see a confirmation, and the till is ready to ring up sales.

4

Add more devices, up to your plan's limit

Repeat steps 2–3 on each extra till. Every device you activate counts against your plan's device limit until it's deactivated — see license status for what happens if you reach it.

5

Let it run

Once activated, Tylliq quietly checks in with our servers in the background to stay current. As long as the device connects to the internet occasionally, there's nothing more for you to do.

6

Replacing or retiring a till

Getting a new device? Open My Devices in the app, deactivate the old one, then activate Tylliq on the new device. The freed-up slot is available immediately — no waiting, no contacting us.

02 — What your status means

Your license is always in one of four states.

Only our team can move a license between them — nothing here changes automatically except becoming Expired, and even that reverses itself the moment it's renewed.

Active

Everything's working normally. Every device on this license can open and use Tylliq without interruption.

Suspended

Temporarily paused — usually a billing or policy hold. Devices show a message and stop working until we lift it. Nothing is deleted, and it can be switched back on as soon as it's resolved.

Revoked

Permanently withdrawn, normally after a serious or repeated issue. Devices stop working, and a new license would be needed to continue using Tylliq.

Expired

Your license's term has ended. Devices pause until it's renewed — the moment it is, your status flips back to Active and your tills pick up where they left off.

03 — Fair use

Staying in good standing.

Four plain-language rules that keep your license healthy. None of them are designed to catch honest shops out.

1

Stay within your device limit

Each license allows a fixed number of active devices. Try to activate one over the limit and Tylliq will tell you: "Your device limit has been reached." Deactivate an unused device to free a slot, or talk to us about a plan with more room.

2

Keep your license key private

Your key is literally what unlocks a device on your account — anyone who has it can activate a till against your license. Share it only within your business, and contact us right away if you think it's leaked; we can issue you a new one.

3

Don't wind back a device's clock

Some people try to cheat time-limited software by turning a device's clock backward. Tylliq notices this. It won't lock you out on the spot — but repeated or large clock rollbacks are flagged on your account for our team to review, and can lead to a manual suspension.

4

Automated setup actions are lightly rate-limited

If a device tries to activate, or your account tries to list or manage devices, far more times than any real shop would in an hour, Tylliq briefly pauses that action as an anti-abuse measure. It resets on its own — just wait about an hour and try again.

05 — Talking to us

If your license is suspended or blocked.

Tylliq doesn't have a self-service "appeal" button today — reinstating a suspended or revoked license is handled personally by our team, not automatically. Suspension never deletes your data: the moment your account is cleared, your license switches back to Active and every device you had picks up right where it left off.

Common reasons a license gets paused:

  • An overdue or failed payment
  • A breach of the terms you agreed to at signup
  • Unusual activity flagged for review, such as repeated clock-tamper warnings

To get it resolved, message us on WhatsApp with your business name and the phone number your account is registered under.

07 — Questions

Frequently asked questions.

If yours isn't here, WhatsApp us.

How many devices can I use with one license? +

As many as your plan's device limit allows. Every device you activate counts against that limit until you deactivate it from My Devices.

Can I move my license to a new computer? +

Yes. Deactivate the old device, then activate Tylliq on the new one. The slot frees up immediately — there's no waiting period and no need to contact us.

What happens if a till loses internet connection? +

Tylliq is built to keep working through short outages and re-syncs your license status automatically once the device is back online.

What actually triggers a suspension? +

Billing issues, a breach of the terms you agreed to, or activity flagged for review — like repeated clock-tamper warnings. A person always reviews it before anything changes, and it's always reversible once resolved.

Is my license key sensitive? +

Yes — treat it like a password. It's the only thing needed to activate a device on your account. If you think it's been shared or leaked, contact us immediately and we'll issue you a new one.
