FAQ

Questions we hear all the time.

If yours isn't here, just WhatsApp us.

Do I pay anything before I see the product? +

No. We build a full clickable prototype of your system at absolutely no cost. You only pay once you've seen it, approved it, and decided you want to proceed. There's no obligation at the prototype stage.

How long does it take to build my system? +

Most systems are delivered in 4–8 weeks from kickoff. The free prototype alone takes about one week. The total timeline depends on the complexity of what you need — we'll give you a specific estimate during our first meeting.

I don't know exactly what I need — is that okay? +

That's exactly what the free consultation is for. Most clients come to us with a problem, not a specification. We ask the right questions, listen carefully, and turn your description of the problem into a concrete system design. You don't need to be technical.

Do you offer payment plans? +

Yes. We work with your budget and find a structure that makes sense. Typically we split payments into 3 milestones: prototype approval, mid-build checkpoint, and final delivery. Reach out and we'll work something out.

What happens after my system launches? +

You get 90 days of post-launch support at no extra cost. That means if anything breaks, doesn't work right, or needs tweaking — we fix it. After 90 days, we offer affordable maintenance packages for ongoing support and updates.

My business is small. Is Vizion right for me? +

Absolutely. Most of our clients are SMEs in Harare that have been running on WhatsApp, Excel, and notebooks. We've built exactly for businesses like yours — and our starting prices reflect that. You don't need a big IT budget to get a proper system.
