Fall armyworm

Maize · sorghum

What you'll see

  • Ragged holes and "windows" in young leaves
  • Sawdust-like droppings in the leaf funnel

What to do

  • Walk the field every week from emergence
  • Crush egg masses and small larvae by hand
  • If damage spreads, ask an agronomist which product to use, and follow the label
