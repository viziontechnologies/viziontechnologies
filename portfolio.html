FleetTrack Pro · Fleet & Rental Management System · Harare, Zimbabwe
Before
  • 18 vehicles tracked via WhatsApp groups and paper logbooks
  • Double bookings happened almost every week
  • Vehicles broke down unexpectedly — no proactive maintenance
  • No way to see monthly revenue without manually adding paper receipts
  • Drivers had no accountability system for mileage or fuel
What we built
  • Web-based booking calendar with real-time conflict prevention
  • Vehicle status board — available, rented, in maintenance
  • Automated maintenance reminders by mileage and schedule
  • Driver management with trip logging and fuel tracking
  • Live financial dashboard showing daily and monthly revenue
