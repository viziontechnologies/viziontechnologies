5 Signs Your Business Needs a POS System
(Not Just Excel)

By Brian Mahove, Vizion Technologies · June 2026 · 5 min read

Most businesses in Harare don't start with Excel by choice — they start with a notebook, migrate to WhatsApp, and eventually land on a spreadsheet because it feels like progress. And for a while, it works. Then it doesn't.

If any of the five signs below sound familiar, your business is losing real money every day you stay on a spreadsheet. Here's what to look for — and what to do about it.

1. You find out your stock levels are wrong only after a customer is already standing in front of you

This is the most common complaint we hear from Harare retailers. You check your spreadsheet in the morning, it says you have 12 units. By afternoon, a customer asks for one and your staff has to physically count the shelf to confirm. The spreadsheet wasn't wrong — someone just forgot to update it. Again.

A proper POS system updates stock automatically the moment a sale happens. No manual entries, no end-of-day reconciling, no guessing. You always know exactly what you have, across every branch, in real time.

2. You can't tell your best-selling product from your worst without sitting down for an hour

Running a business without knowing your top sellers is like driving without a speedometer. You can feel like things are going well and still be quietly bleeding from slow-moving stock that's tying up your cash.

With a POS system, your best sellers, your worst performers, your peak sales hours, and your profit margins are a single click away — updated after every transaction. No report-building required.

3. Your staff can give themselves discounts or void sales without you knowing

This one is painful to talk about, but it's real. If you're using a spreadsheet or a manual receipt book, there's almost no way to catch this. A staff member rings up a sale, gives an unauthorised discount, voids a line item, or simply doesn't record a cash transaction at all — and your spreadsheet will never know.

A POS system logs every transaction with a timestamp, a staff login, and a full audit trail. Discounts require manager approval. Voids are flagged. It doesn't assume your staff are dishonest — it just makes dishonesty impossible to hide.

4. Your month-end is a nightmare of reconciling paper with reality

If closing out the month means two or three people cross-referencing WhatsApp messages, paper receipts, and an Excel sheet to figure out what actually happened — you've built a job that didn't need to exist. That's hours every month that should be going toward growing your business.

A POS system closes your day, week, and month automatically. Your sales summary, cash drawer reconciliation, and tax totals are ready when you need them. Month-end goes from a half-day process to a five-minute review.

5. You've had to say "I'm not sure" when someone asked how much your business made last month

If you can't answer that question confidently within 60 seconds, that's a red flag. Not because you're doing something wrong — but because you're operating without the visibility you need to make good decisions. Do you expand? Do you restock? Do you hire? You can't answer any of those questions well if you don't know your numbers.

The businesses we work with consistently tell us that the single biggest unlock from getting proper software is this: they finally know what's happening in their business. Not a rough estimate. Not a vague feeling. Real numbers, right there.
